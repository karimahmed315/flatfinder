Add a flat to the hub

Paste a link from Rightmove, Zoopla, OpenRent, Share to Buy or an agent's site. It'll show up straight away as a pending card. Then hit Copy to share and send the summary over (WhatsApp, email, however) so the full details — price, beds, photo — get filled in and it becomes a permanent listing.

Curated from public listings on Rightmove, Zoopla and Share to Buy (the Mayor of London's official affordable-housing portal). Prices, availability and share percentages change often — always confirm details, lease length, service charges and eligibility directly with the agent or housing provider before acting. Shared-ownership rent is estimated at ~2.5%/yr of the unsold share; the provider's actual rate may differ.
